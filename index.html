About

I'm an economist by training and a risk analyst by profession. I studied a five-year integrated M.Sc. in Economics, interned in the Economic Policy and Research Department at NSE India, and now work on credit risk data at Societe Generale. What pulls me in is how economic principles show up in the real world, in finance, policy and data.

Four words I keep coming back to, and the parts of me each one points to.

Curious mind
I default to asking why before I decide what I think. Unfamiliar ideas don't unsettle me, they pull me in, and I'd rather sit with a good question than rush to a tidy answer.
Competitive spirit
I notice the scoreboard, even when nobody else is keeping one. I measure myself against where I was yesterday, and I genuinely enjoy the push of trying to close that gap.
Slightly delusional
I assume most things are possible until someone shows me the evidence, and then I ask for a second opinion. A bit of unreasonable belief is usually what gets things started.
Heavily ambitious
I'm not in a hurry, but I'm not planning to stay put either. I think in terms of what's next, and I'm comfortable being early, unfinished, or slightly out of my depth.
